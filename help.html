Frequently Asked Questions

Click on any question below to instantly view details and guidelines

What documents do I need to rent a car in the UK? +
You will need a valid driver's license (UK photocard or international equivalent), a passport or official ID, and a credit card under the primary driver's name for security deposit clearance.
Is comprehensive insurance included in the daily rate? +
Yes, standard collision damage waiver (CDW) and theft protection are included automatically. You can also opt for zero-excess coverage during final online checkout.
Can I modify or cancel my vehicle reservation for free? +
Absolute flexibility: we offer 100% free modifications and cancellations up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled pick-up time at any UK hub.
Are electric vehicles supplied with a full battery and charging cable? +
Yes, all EVs (such as our Tesla Model Y fleet) are delivered with a 100% state of charge and include full access kits and charging adapters for UK public networks.
